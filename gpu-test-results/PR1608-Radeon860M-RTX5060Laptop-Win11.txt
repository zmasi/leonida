Check of PR #1518, separate from the baseline results
Both adapters reproduce the cs_imgatomic_ext mismatch already reported in #1608 and #14 and fixed by open PR #1518 (PierreEbele). The 3D texture kept its fill values.
#1518 changes the fixture's volume T# words to s12 = 0x80000055 and s15 = 0xd7801800, the key vk_store_test expects since db19bbd34. With those two values applied in a separate worktree, cs_imgatomic_ext passed 1/1 on both adapters in both profiles. This confirms #1518 on Radeon 860M and RTX 5060 Laptop. It is not a separate fix. The baseline checkout is clean.
