          About
          
        
          
          
          Leonida is a compatibility runtime that runs your own copy of the Xbox Series build of GTA VI on a PC: a loader that maps the
             game's executable and a Direct3D 12 layer that draws its frames on your graphics card.
          
          
          It is an unofficial, independent project. It is not made, endorsed or supported by the game's publisher, and it contains none of
             the game: you build it from a copy you own, and the game itself never goes online. The picture behind PLAY is the
             game's key art, fetched once from rockstargames.com and kept on this PC, unless you choose your own in Settings; the logo is the repository's assets/logo.svg.
